  <read_first>src/ndd-change-intake.cts, .planning/phases/02-change-intake-and-spec-drafting/02-CONTEXT.md</read_first>

  <acceptance_criteria>

    - Ambiguous/conflicting items retain source references.
    - The spec remains draft and does not mark the change as approved.
  </acceptance_criteria>
  <done>Messy source documents are surfaced honestly for Phase 4 discussion.</done>
</task>
