# Roadmap: NDD Brownfield Change Workflow

## Overview

NDD is a shipped brownfield change workflow layered on top of GSD Core. v1.0 established the fixed `ndd` namespace, Markdown change intake, impact discovery, discussion/approval, and adapters that bridge approved NDD changes into GSD planning, execution, verification, and ship mechanics.
